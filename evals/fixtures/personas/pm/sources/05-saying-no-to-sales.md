---
title: Saying no to a sales-driven feature request
source_type: personal_note
memory_context: observation
---
Sales asked for a custom intake form builder because a prospect said they would sign if we had it. The prospect was a hospital group, far bigger than our usual clinics.

I said no, but with numbers: it was roughly eight weeks of work, the prospect was one deal, and only 3 percent of support tickets mentioned intake forms. Northgate Health, our biggest real customer, had never asked for it.

What I offered instead: a PDF upload field, two days of work. The prospect did not sign. Sales was annoyed for a quarter. Nobody has asked for the form builder since. Saying no is part of the job; saying no without data is just an opinion.
