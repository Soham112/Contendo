---
title: Replacing long PRDs with one-page briefs
source_type: personal_note
memory_context: observation
---
Observation: our twelve-page PRDs were read by the author and one reviewer. Engineers skimmed the first page. So the first page is now the whole document: problem, evidence, who it is for, what we will not do, and how we will know it worked. Details live in tickets. Review comments went up, not down.
