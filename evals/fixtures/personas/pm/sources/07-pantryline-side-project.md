Pantryline is a meal planning app I built for my family with a no-code tool. It picks five dinners a week from recipes we like and makes a shopping list.

My partner ignored the first version. The second version added a single feature: a "we're tired" button that swaps any dinner for something under 20 minutes. Usage went from zero to every week.

Building for one household is humbling: no metrics to hide behind, just whether it gets used on a Tuesday.
