Clinic Pulse numbers at 90 days. 44 percent of paying Brightloom clinics opened it at least once a month. The weekly summary email had a 58 percent open rate and drove most of the visits.

Clinics that used Clinic Pulse monthly churned at 1.1 percent a month versus 2.4 percent for those that did not. Correlation, not proof: engaged clinics are engaged everywhere.

Northgate Health used the no-show chart to justify a deposit policy and cut no-shows from 9 to 5 percent. That one story did more for adoption than any in-app prompt: we added it to onboarding emails and monthly opens rose six points.
