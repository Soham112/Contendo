Northgate Health is a fictional chain of six physiotherapy clinics and one of Brightloom's largest customers. I spent two days at their front desks.

What I saw: receptionists keep a paper list of patients who want earlier appointments, and phone them when someone cancels. They called it "the shuffle." It took about 40 minutes a day per clinic and nobody had ever mentioned it in a feature request, because they did not think software could do it.

Three things I wrote down: people describe workarounds, not needs; the most valuable problem is often the one they have stopped complaining about; sit where the work happens, do not just book a video call.

This became the waitlist feature. It also changed how I run discovery: at least one on-site day per quarter.
