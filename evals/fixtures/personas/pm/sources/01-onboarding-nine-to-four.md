---
title: Cutting Brightloom onboarding from nine steps to four
source_type: personal_note
memory_context: work
---
Brightloom onboarding had nine steps: clinic details, locations, practitioners, services, opening hours, booking rules, reminders, payments, and calendar sync. Only 38 percent of new clinics finished setup within 14 days, and clinics that did not finish almost never converted from trial.

Discovery calls kept surfacing the same sentence, from a clinic manager at Northgate Health: "I don't have time to set up software that is supposed to save me time."

What we did: kept four required steps (clinic, practitioners, services, hours), filled sensible defaults for booking rules and reminders, and moved payments and calendar sync to after the first booking. Two of the steps we cut were ones I had argued for in the original design.

Result after eight weeks: 14-day setup completion went from 38 to 61 percent. Trial-to-paid moved from 11 to 15 percent. Support tickets tagged "setup" fell by a third.

Lesson: the activation problem was a setup problem wearing a growth costume.
