---
title: Per-seat versus per-location pricing experiment
source_type: personal_note
memory_context: work
---
Brightloom priced per practitioner seat. Clinics hated adding part-time practitioners because each one cost the same as a full-timer, so they shared logins, which broke our reporting.

We tested per-location pricing for new sign-ups for ten weeks, with a price set so the average clinic would pay about the same.

Results: conversion improved from 13 to 16 percent; average revenue per account fell 4 percent; shared logins in the test group dropped to almost zero, which made Clinic Pulse data trustworthy. Large multi-practitioner clinics paid less, small single-location clinics paid slightly more.

We moved to per-location pricing with a practitioner cap per tier. The decision was less about revenue and more about removing the incentive to share logins.
