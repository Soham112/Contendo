title: Raising the Saltmarsh Labs seed round
source_type: personal_note
memory_context: work
---
Saltmarsh Labs raised a 1.6 million euro seed round, led by Harbor Ventures, a fictional fund. It took 41 investor meetings over eleven weeks. Thirty-eight said no.

The most common objection was "API mocking is a feature, not a company." What changed the conversation was usage data: 23 teams using Driftwood weekly, and four of them had replaced an internal tool with it.

Harbor Ventures' partner asked a question nobody else did: "What would make you shut this down?" I answered honestly, with a churn number and a date. She said that was why they invested.

What I would do differently: run all first meetings in two weeks, not eleven. Momentum is a real thing, and a slow process made every meeting feel like a first meeting.
