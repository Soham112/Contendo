---
title: How Driftwood got its first ten paying customers
source_type: personal_note
memory_context: work
---
The first ten paying Driftwood customers all came from founder-led sales. Not one came from the website.

Where they came from: four from my old network at Quayside Payments, three from a post in a backend engineering forum about how we mocked a payments API, two from conference hallway conversations, and one from a cold email that I sent to 60 people.

The pattern: every customer had a specific painful integration, usually a third-party API with a slow or flaky sandbox. Driftwood was a cure for that pain, not a general tool. Once I started opening calls with "which sandbox do you hate most?" my demo-to-trial rate roughly doubled.

I did all 40 demos myself before hiring anyone for sales.
