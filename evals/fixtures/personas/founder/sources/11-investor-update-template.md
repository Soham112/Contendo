The monthly investor update I send to Harbor Ventures and our angels.

Sections, in order: default alive date and runway in months; revenue, growth rate, and burn; Driftwood usage (weekly active teams, and how many replaced an internal tool); what went wrong this month; what we decided; specific asks, each with a name next to it.

Rule: the "what went wrong" section is never empty. Investors who see problems early help early. Since Saltmarsh Labs started sending this format, the number of useful intros from investors went from about one a quarter to three a month.
