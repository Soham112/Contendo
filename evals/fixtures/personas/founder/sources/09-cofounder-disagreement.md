---
title: Co-founder disagreement about an enterprise pivot
source_type: personal_note
memory_context: observation
---
My co-founder wanted to pivot toward enterprise after two large companies asked for on-premises Driftwood. I wanted to stay with mid-size teams.

We almost argued about it for a month. What resolved it was writing down what each of us would need to believe for the other to be right, then checking. Enterprise required a sales hire we could not afford for at least nine months. We agreed to revisit at the next round and offer one enterprise pilot at a high price. Disagreement got easier when it became a list of testable beliefs.
