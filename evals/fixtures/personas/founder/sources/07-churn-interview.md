title: Churn interview with a team that left Driftwood
source_type: personal_note
memory_context: work
---
A 12-engineer fintech team cancelled Driftwood after seven months. I asked for a 20-minute call and they said yes.

Their reason was not price or bugs. Their main third-party payment provider finally shipped a reliable sandbox, so their biggest reason for mocking disappeared.

What this taught me: Driftwood's value is tied to how bad other companies' sandboxes are. That is a fragile moat. We are now building contract testing on top of the mocks, so Driftwood stays useful even when sandboxes improve.

I now do a churn call for every cancelled team. About half agree.
