---
title: Preparing for board meetings with Harbor Ventures
source_type: personal_note
memory_context: work
---
Our board is me, my co-founder, and a partner from Harbor Ventures. Early meetings were me presenting slides for an hour.

What works now: a four-page memo sent three days before, covering numbers, what went wrong, what we decided, and the one question I want help with. The meeting itself is 30 minutes of discussion on that question. The partner from Harbor Ventures said it was the first board pack she had read fully in months.

Writing the memo is the useful part. It forces me to admit what is not working before anyone asks.
