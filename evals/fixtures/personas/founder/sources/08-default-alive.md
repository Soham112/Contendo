---
title: Notes on default alive versus default dead
source_type: article
memory_context: learning
---
Learning notes. A startup is "default alive" if, at current growth and expense rates, it reaches profitability before the money runs out. Otherwise it is "default dead" and depends on raising again.

The calculation needs three numbers: current monthly revenue, revenue growth rate, and monthly burn. I now put this calculation at the top of every investor update.

Why it matters to me: at my first company we never did the maths and raised in a panic. It changes behaviour when everyone can see the date.
