---
title: Hiring the first engineer at Saltmarsh Labs
source_type: personal_note
memory_context: work
---
Our first engineering hire at Saltmarsh Labs took four months. I interviewed 26 people.

I first optimised for raw technical skill and nearly hired someone brilliant who wanted a detailed spec for everything. At four people, nobody writes specs.

What I switched to: a paid two-day project working on a real Driftwood bug, with me in the same chat. I learned more from how candidates asked questions than from how they coded.

The person we hired was not the strongest coder in the pool. She was the one who shipped a fix, wrote a note to a customer, and asked why the bug existed in the first place. The first engineer is a culture decision dressed as a technical one.
