---
title: Repricing Driftwood from per-user to team pricing
source_type: personal_note
memory_context: work
---
Driftwood launched at 9 euros per user per month. It looked cheap and friendly. The data said otherwise: users on that plan had low activity and churned at 9 percent a month, while teams that asked for invoices used it daily.

We moved to team pricing: 290 euros a month for up to 15 engineers, and an enterprise tier. Sign-ups fell by about 40 percent. Monthly revenue rose 60 percent within a quarter, and churn dropped to 3 percent.

The lesson I keep repeating to other founders: a low price attracts people who are not sure they have the problem. Saltmarsh Labs wants people who are sure.
