Our own figures for Q3 2026 (July to September), from our sales plus Land Registry data for the Kelso Vale postcodes. Average sold price 352,000 pounds, up 3.1% on Q3 2025. Average time from listing to agreed offer 41 days, down from 48. Three-bedroom family homes made up half of all sales.
