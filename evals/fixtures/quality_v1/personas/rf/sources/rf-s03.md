Six-week pilot with Brightline Insurance (their support team of 22 agents), ended August 2026. Brightline agreed in writing that we can share these numbers publicly.

Before the pilot their assistant resolved 18% of chats without a human. After six weekly eval-and-fix cycles with Sieve it resolved 31%. The biggest single gain came from week 3, when the eval showed the assistant refused to answer anything containing the word "claim", even simple status questions.

Their support lead, Marisol, said the useful part was not the score but the list of failing conversations every Monday.
