---
title: Moving Sieve from per-seat to usage pricing
source_type: personal_note
memory_context: work
authorship: user_authored
---
In Q1 2026 we moved Sieve from per-seat pricing to a price per evaluated conversation. Two of our nine customers left within a month; both had large teams and low volume, so the new price was higher for them.

ARR was flat for the whole quarter. In Q2 it grew 40%, mostly from existing customers evaluating more conversations once each one had a visible price.

What I got wrong: I announced it by email with two weeks' notice. The two customers who left both said the notice period, not the price, was why they did not try to make it work.
