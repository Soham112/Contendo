Our first engineering hire took eleven weeks. The strongest candidate on a whiteboard wrote one-line pull request descriptions in the take-home. I said no, which felt wrong at the time.

The engineer we hired, Tomasz, wrote PR descriptions that explained what he tried and rejected. On a team of three working across time zones, that turned out to be the job. Eighteen months later most of our onboarding docs started as his PR descriptions.
