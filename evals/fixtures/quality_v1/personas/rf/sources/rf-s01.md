Sieve started from fourteen discovery calls with support leads in early 2025. Every one of them had put an AI assistant in front of customers, and every one of them had the same problem: after a prompt or model change, nobody could say whether answers had got better or worse. They found out from angry tickets.

Quillmark's first version of Sieve was a spreadsheet. I took 200 real tickets from one design partner, wrote the answer a good agent would give, and compared the assistant's replies by hand every Friday. It took me six hours a week. That was the moment I knew the product was the comparison, not the dashboard.
