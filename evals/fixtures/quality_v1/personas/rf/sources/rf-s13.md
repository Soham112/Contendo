A conference talk on evaluating retrieval-augmented generation. The speaker separates retrieval metrics (recall at k, precision) from answer metrics (faithfulness, relevance) and argues most teams only measure the second. Recommends building a small labelled set from production queries, tracking it in CI, and reviewing failures weekly.
