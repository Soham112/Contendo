title: Evalyze raises 20 million dollar Series A for LLM testing
source_type: article
memory_context: learning
---
Evalyze, a startup that sells automated testing for language model applications, announced a 20 million dollar Series A. The company says it has more than 300 customers and plans to expand into regulated industries. Investors cited the growing number of companies putting AI assistants in front of customers without a way to test them.
