---
title: LLM-as-a-judge: a survey of known biases
source_type: article
memory_context: learning
---
This survey reviews research on using large language models to evaluate other models' outputs. Reported problems include position bias (preferring the first answer shown), verbosity bias (preferring longer answers) and self-preference (rating outputs from the same model family higher). The authors recommend randomising answer order, calibrating against human labels and reporting agreement rates rather than raw scores.
