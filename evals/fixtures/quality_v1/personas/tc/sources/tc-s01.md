Engagement notes, spring 2026. A mid-size packaging manufacturer (client asked not to be named) was losing most of a shift each week to line changeovers. We filmed three changeovers and found that 40% of the time went to looking for tools and settings sheets. Moving the tooling to shadow boards at each line and printing settings on the machine guard cut average changeover time from 74 to 58 minutes in six weeks, about 22%. The operators designed the boards; I mostly asked questions.
