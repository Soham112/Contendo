A review of published case studies on machine-learning demand forecasting. Reported forecast error improvements range from 5% to 30%, mostly at retailers with long, clean sales histories. The author notes that mid-size manufacturers with lumpy B2B orders rarely see the same gains and often lack the data quality to start.
