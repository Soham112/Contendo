Section 3: Account opening

Across the 14 business banking apps we reviewed, the median signup form had 11 fields and the median completion rate was 54%. Apps that allowed applicants to defer document lookups completed 9 points higher on average than those that required every field up front.

Respondents most often abandoned at the company registration step. The report recommends deferring any field that requires a document the applicant is unlikely to have open.
