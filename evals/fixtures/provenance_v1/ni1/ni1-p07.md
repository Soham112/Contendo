I have redesigned the onboarding flow at three fintech startups, and every time the first version asked for too much before showing anything useful.

At my last company we cut the sign-up form from nine fields to three and moved identity checks after the first deposit screen. Completion went from 38% to 61% in six weeks.

Show people the product before you ask them to prove who they are.

— Tomás Ruiz, design lead
