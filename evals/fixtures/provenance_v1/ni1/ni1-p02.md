Everyone tells you to cut form fields. I did, and our conversion went down.

I lead design at a payroll startup. Last year we cut our signup form from ten fields to five. Completion went up 12%, and then activation fell, because the five fields we removed were the ones that set up payroll correctly on day one. People finished signup and then got stuck.

My takeaway: measure the step after the form, not the form. A shorter form that moves the work to later is not a better form.

Written by Lea Fontaine, design lead at Paycrest
