The card settings screen I shipped last month took three rounds of testing. The first version put freeze, limits and PIN reset on one page, and five of six testers could not find the freeze toggle.

Round two split them into tabs, which tested worse. Round three kept one page but put freeze at the top on its own, and every tester found it in under ten seconds.

The lesson I keep relearning: test the one action people need in a panic before anything else.

Ren Takahashi
