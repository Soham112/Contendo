Talk by the head of design at a neobank. Main points:

Progressive onboarding beats long forms when the product can be useful before setup is complete. They let people open an account with three fields and asked for the rest when it was first needed.

Their numbers: account opening up 30%, but a third of new accounts were never fully verified. They now nudge at day three with a single-field request.
