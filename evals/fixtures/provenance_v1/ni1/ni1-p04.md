For two years I designed onboarding for a business bank, and the field that cost us the most signups was the company registration number.

People did not abandon the form because it was long. They abandoned it because they had to leave the screen to find a document. When we let them skip that one field and add it later, completion rose by a fifth.

So now, before I cut any field, I ask which ones send people away from the screen.

Sofia Brandt, product designer
