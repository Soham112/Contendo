Looking back at our signup form work. We cut twelve fields to six and completion went up, but I think we drew the wrong lesson from it.

It was not the number of fields. The six we removed were the ones that asked for things people had to go and find, like the company registration number. When I tested a version that kept twelve fields but let people skip the lookups until later, completion was almost the same.

Next time I would test which fields send people away from the screen before cutting anything.
