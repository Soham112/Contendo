Hi, I'm Ren, I design onboarding for a small-business banking app, and this talk is about a mistake we nearly repeated.

We cut our signup form from twelve fields to six and completion went up, so we told everyone shorter forms win. Then I tested a version that kept all twelve but let people skip the lookups, like the registration number, until later. Completion was almost the same.

So the lesson was never the number of fields. It was which fields made people leave the screen to go and find something.
