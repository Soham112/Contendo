House prices in the Kelso Vale rose about 3% over the year to September, ahead of the national figure, according to Land Registry data compiled for this review.

Local agents report that three-bedroom family homes remain the most sought after. Fernleigh Homes, Brookfield Estates and two online agencies accounted for most listings in Ashby.

Planning permission for up to 120 homes east of Mill Lane is expected to add supply from 2028.
