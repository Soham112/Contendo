The Courier asked Fernleigh Homes, the family-run agency on Market Street, how the Kelso Vale market is holding up.

We sold more three-bedroom family homes this year than anything else, about half of all our sales. Homes that are priced right in the first two weeks still sell quickly, and the ones that are not tend to sit. We tell every seller that, and most of them now listen.

What would you tell a first-time buyer looking in Ashby this winter?

Come to the viewings yourself and walk the riverside path to the station before you make an offer. It takes ten minutes from Mill Lane, and the train to the city is 38. We go to every viewing with buyers, so ask us anything on the day.
