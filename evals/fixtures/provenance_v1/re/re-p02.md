At Brookfield Estates we have sold homes in the Kelso Vale for over thirty years, and we always tell sellers the same five things.

First, ask three agents and ignore the highest valuation. Second, price to the nearest round number buyers search by. Third, spring and early autumn are still the best months to list. Fourth, kerb appeal matters more than kitchens. Fifth, never accept the first offer in the first week without a second viewing.
