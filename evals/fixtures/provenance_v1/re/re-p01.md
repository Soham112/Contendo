Fernleigh Homes autumn newsletter

It has been a busy quarter in the Kelso Vale. Homes we sold took an average of 41 days to reach an agreed offer, a week quicker than last year, and three-bedroom family homes made up half of everything we sold.

Our advice to anyone thinking of selling before Christmas: price for the first two weeks. Most of our sales agreed in the first fourteen days, and the homes we relisted lower after a month took longer than if they had started there.

As always, we accompany every viewing ourselves. Call the office on 01632 960 123 for a free valuation.
