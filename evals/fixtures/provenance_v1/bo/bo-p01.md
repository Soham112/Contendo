Frequently asked questions

Do you come to me? Yes. We repair bikes at your home or workplace anywhere in North Ledbury, from one van with one mechanic.

How soon can you come? Usually the same week. Text us the bike and the problem and we will reply with a time.

Do you check e-bikes? Yes. An e-bike check is 30 pounds on top of a basic or full service.
