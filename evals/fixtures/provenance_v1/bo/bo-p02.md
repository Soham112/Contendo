For twelve months I did every bit of maintenance on my e-bike myself, to see whether a home mechanic can keep up with the extra weight and torque.

Brake pads were the first thing to go, at about 800 miles, twice as fast as on my old road bike. The chain stretched by the six-month mark. The motor itself never needed anything.

My advice after a year: check pads monthly, budget for two chains a year, and leave the motor to a specialist.
