Strong Over 40: class guide

Our Strong Over 40 sessions run Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 9.30am, capped at eight people. Ana, our head coach, takes most of them.

Every session starts with fifteen minutes of mobility, then one main lift, usually the trap-bar deadlift or the goblet squat, then carries. We progress the weight only when the last set looks as good as the first. Nobody tests a one-rep max in their first three months with us.
