The new industry report says 41% of support leaders have no systematic way to measure answer quality. I think the real number is higher, because the ones who say they do usually mean a CSAT score.

In every Sieve onboarding this year I have asked to see the last ten failed conversations a team reviewed. Most teams could not show me one. A score is not a review, and I would put that in the report's first line if I wrote it.
