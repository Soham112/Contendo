I have built eval suites for support assistants for four years, and the hardest lesson was that a golden set tells you about the tickets you chose, not the ones customers send.

At my last company our suite passed at 96% the week our assistant started promising refunds it could not give. Not one golden ticket mentioned refunds. We rebuilt the set from a weighted sample of live traffic and caught three regressions in the next quarter.

If your eval set was written by the people who wrote the prompt, it is testing their imagination.

Noor Haddad, staff engineer, Lumen Support
