Welcome back. Today I'm talking to Dara Okafor, who founded Quillmark. Dara, why did you start with a spreadsheet instead of software?

Because the first version had to be the comparison, not the dashboard. I took 200 real tickets from one design partner, wrote the answer a good agent would give, and compared replies by hand every Friday. It took me six hours a week, and that pain told me exactly what to automate first.

And what would you tell a support lead who has no evals at all?

Ask what your assistant must never say. Write those down. That list is your first eval, and it is usually ten lines long, not a thousand. Everything else can come once you are reading failures every week.
