Six years on ICU night shifts taught me that checklists only work when the person using them helped write them.

When our unit brought in a new handover checklist from head office, compliance was under half within a month. We sat down as a team, cut it from 30 items to 11, and added the two things that had actually gone wrong on our ward that year. Compliance went above 90% and stayed there.

I think about that every time someone hands a nurse a form designed by someone who has never worked a night.
