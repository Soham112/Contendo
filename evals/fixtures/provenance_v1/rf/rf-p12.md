At Brightline our support assistant answered a lot of questions confidently and wrongly. In spring we ran a six-week pilot with Sieve, Quillmark's evaluation tool.

Every Monday we received a list of failing conversations. In week three it showed our assistant refused any message that contained the word "claim", which for an insurer is most of them. We fixed the prompt in two days.

By the end of the pilot our assistant resolved 31% of chats without an agent, up from 18%. The bigger change was cultural: our team now reads failures every week instead of watching a score.

Marisol Vega, Head of Support, Brightline Insurance
