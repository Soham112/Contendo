Our first engineering hire took eleven weeks, and the decision came down to pull request descriptions.

The strongest whiteboard candidate wrote one-line PR descriptions in the take-home. Tomasz wrote ones that explained what he tried and why he rejected it. On a team of three across time zones, I realised that was the job.

Eighteen months later, most of Quillmark's onboarding docs started life as his PR descriptions. I still read the PR descriptions in a take-home before I read the code.
