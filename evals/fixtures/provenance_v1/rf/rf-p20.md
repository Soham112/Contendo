Guest post by Dara Okafor, founder of Quillmark

In the first quarter of this year I moved Sieve from per-seat pricing to a price per evaluated conversation. Two of our nine customers left within a month, both with large teams and low volume.

Revenue was flat for a quarter and then grew 40%, mostly from existing customers evaluating more once each conversation had a visible price.

The part I got wrong was the notice. I gave two weeks by email. Both customers who left told me the notice period, not the price, was why they did not try to make it work.
