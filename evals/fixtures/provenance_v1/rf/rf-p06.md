Ruiz argues that most teams measure what is cheap to observe rather than what customers experience. Her example is call centres that optimised handle time and watched satisfaction fall for two years before anyone connected the two.

Her fix is a weekly review of a small random sample of real interactions, read by the people who can change the system, with no score attached. She says the score comes later, once you know what you are looking for, and that teams who start with a dashboard rarely look past it.

We tried the no-score review at Quillmark for a month. The first Monday we found the assistant refusing every message with the word claim in it.
