I changed my mind about model judges this year. We ran one next to two human reviewers on 500 conversations and it agreed with them 86% of the time, about as often as they agreed with each other.

"Reported problems include position bias, verbosity bias and self-preference; we recommend randomising answer order and calibrating against human labels." (Liang and Moreau, 2025)

That paper is why we still put a human on a random 10% of conversations every week. A judge without an audit is a guess with good manners, and I would rather know when ours drifts than find out from a customer.
