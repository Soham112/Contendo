Last spring I moved my company from per-seat pricing to usage pricing and it almost broke us.

We had 40 customers at Tallyhand. Eleven of them had big teams and tiny volume, and every one of those eleven saw their bill go up. Six left within the quarter. Revenue fell 18% before it recovered.

What I would do differently: talk to the eleven first, one by one, before any email goes out. We announced it in a newsletter. That was the mistake, not the price.

Priya Natarajan, founder of Tallyhand
