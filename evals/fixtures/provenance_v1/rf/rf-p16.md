Read the Andreessen piece on AI in customer support this morning.

Their argument: support is the first enterprise function where AI agents will do most of the work, because the tasks are bounded and the data already exists. They expect resolution rates above 60% within three years for companies that invest in evaluation and retrieval.

They also say the winners will own the workflow, not the model, and that evaluation tools will be absorbed into larger platforms.
