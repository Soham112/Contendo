Last year my team triaged just over 40,000 support tickets by hand before we let a model touch any of them. I wanted every agent to know what a good first reply looked like before we automated one.

The surprise was how few categories mattered. Eleven ticket types covered 80% of volume, and most of the hard ones were billing questions that needed a human anyway.

If you are about to put AI in front of your customers, label your own tickets first. You will learn more in a month of that than from any vendor demo.

Lena Brandt, Head of Support at Corvel
