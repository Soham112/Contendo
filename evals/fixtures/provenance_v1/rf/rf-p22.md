Natarajan's talk was about switching pricing models without losing customers. Her main claim: segment customers by how the new price changes their bill, and call every customer whose bill goes up before anything is announced.

She showed that Tallyhand lost six of eleven customers whose bills rose when they announced by newsletter, and none in a later change when they called each one first. She also recommended a full quarter of notice and a grandfathered rate for a year.

For Sieve: next time we change anything about price, I call every customer whose bill rises before the email goes out.
