Quillmark, the London startup behind the AI evaluation tool Sieve, has signed Brightline Insurance as its first enterprise customer, the company confirmed this week.

The deal follows a six-week pilot in which Brightline's support assistant went from resolving 18% of chats without a human to 31%. "The score mattered less than the list of failing conversations every Monday," said Dara Okafor, Quillmark's founder and chief executive.

Quillmark raised a 700,000 dollar pre-seed round from angel investors in 2024. It competes with better-funded rivals such as Evalyze, which raised a 20 million dollar Series A this year. Analysts at Gartwell Research expect the market for AI quality tooling to double by 2028, driven by insurers and banks that face regulatory pressure over automated customer answers.
