This month Plant Notes spoke to Jonah Whitlock, an independent consultant who works with mid-size manufacturers. Why start with changeovers?

Because in a high-mix plant that is where the hours go. On my last engagement we filmed three changeovers and found that about 40% of the time was spent looking for tools and settings sheets. Nobody needed a new method; they needed the tools where the work happens.

What do clients get wrong?

They bring in standard work from a car plant and expect it to hold on a line that runs two hundred variants. I would rather start with flow and changeovers and let the operators design the fixes, because the ones they design are still there a year later.
