The Midlands Manufacturing Council has announced the shortlist for its 2026 lean improvement awards. Twelve projects were shortlisted from 61 entries.

Among them is a changeover reduction project at an unnamed packaging manufacturer, supported by Whitlock Operations, an independent consultancy run by Jonah Whitlock. Judges noted the project's 22% cut in changeover time and the fact that line operators designed the tooling boards themselves.

Winners will be announced at the council's dinner in November. Last year's overall award went to a foundry in Walsall for a scrap reduction programme.
