I have run SMED workshops for twenty years, in bakeries, print shops and car plants, and I have learned to distrust any project that starts with a stopwatch and ends with a binder.

The bakery project I am proudest of cut a 90-minute changeover to 35. We did it by moving the mixing bowls onto trolleys, not by writing procedures. The operators found most of the savings in the first afternoon.

My advice to younger consultants: let the people on the line own the result. If they did not design it, it will be gone in six months.

Martina Lopez, Lopez Lean Consulting
