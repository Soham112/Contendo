Changeover review: interim findings
Prepared by Jonah Whitlock, Whitlock Operations

Over three weeks I filmed and timed three changeovers on lines 2 and 4. Roughly 40% of each changeover went to finding tools, settings sheets and the right size of guide rail.

My recommendation for the next six weeks is shadow boards at each line, with settings printed on the machine guard, designed by the operators rather than by me. I would not start with standard work: the line runs too many variants for a fixed sequence to hold.

I will re-time the same changeovers in week six and report the change against today's average of 74 minutes.
