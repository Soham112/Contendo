Hello everyone, I'm Jonah Whitlock and this is the second of my short webinars on running changeovers in plants that make hundreds of variants.

Most of what I see in high-mix plants is lean tools copied from car factories. Takt time and levelled schedules assume volume you do not have. On my last engagement we ignored all of that and started with one question: where does the time go during a changeover? The answer was looking for things, about 40% of it.

So today I will show you how we filmed three changeovers, what the shadow boards looked like, and why I let the operators design them instead of drawing them myself.
