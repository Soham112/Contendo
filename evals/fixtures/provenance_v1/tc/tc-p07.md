Every changeover project I run ends with shadow boards, and every client thinks the boards are the result. They are not.

The result is that the operators spent a week arguing about where each tool should live. I have seen beautiful boards fail in a month because an engineer drew them, and scruffy ones last for years because the night shift made them.

Twenty-two years in, I still learn more on the line than in the office.

Ravi Mehta, lean coach
